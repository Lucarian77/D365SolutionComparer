# Process and workflow comparison

Process membership and definition results are separate. Local record identifiers are used only for correlation within an environment and do not establish cross-environment identity.

## Identity and uncertainty

A nonblank workflow unique name is the preferred portable key. A verified activation may refer to its definition through the retained parent relationship. Where a supported fallback is used, the framed candidate combines the name, definition type, category, primary entity and applicable subtype. Equal display names alone do not establish identity.

The fallback is a solution-scoped semantic correlation rule, not a documented globally unique alternate key or proof of component lineage. Duplicate complete candidates remain ambiguous. Missing or conflicting metadata and incomplete coverage prevent unsupported absence conclusions. Different identity strategies are not silently paired. Unavailable coverage is distinct from a confirmed absent solution.

## Supported definition evidence

Supported configuration fields include type, category, primary entity, mode, subprocess and applicable business-process, modern-flow or UI-flow subtype properties. Required metadata and values must be available before a configuration match is established. Inapplicable subtype values are distinct from missing required evidence.

This is configuration comparison. Workflow execution payloads are outside this contract; no full execution-logic equality is implied. Identity values and local audit identifiers do not automatically become definition-equality fields.

## Reading results

Review retained side-owned identity, coverage and property evidence. Preserve the distinction between confirmed agreement, difference, absence and uncertainty. Do not force counts or outcomes to match an earlier environment snapshot. This document contains no environment-specific examples or live-testing instructions.
